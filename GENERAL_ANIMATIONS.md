# Lucas Essential Animation Library

A starter collection of **25 animation groups** selected from the Lucas assets for reuse in character-based games. It covers attention, movement, expressions, gestures, resting, a simple object interaction, and celebration. Choose the actions that fit your game.

All animation names preserve their original spelling. Related left and right walking clips share a group, giving **27 source clips** in total.

**Project root:** `/Users/rax/Desktop/lucas`

Skeleton file paths are relative to this root. Animation names identify clips inside the `.skel` file; they are not separate files.
